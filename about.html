Timber Anatomy

The Living Grain of the
Zambezi River Basin.

African Mukwa (Pterocarpus angolensis) is revered for its high resin density, flame retardance, and total natural immunity to wood-borer insects. Its golden-amber heartwood possesses an iridescent depth that deepens with age.

Botanical Species Pterocarpus angolensis
Janka Hardness 1,260 lbf
Timber Density 660 kg/m³
Surface Luster Hand-Rubbed Carnauba Wax

A dining table or presidential desk is where decisions of state and family legacies are cast. Each Royal Decaux commission is hand-finished with natural carnauba wax to preserve the breathing timber grain.
